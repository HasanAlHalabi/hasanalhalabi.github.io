The Fresh Graduate's Career Roadmap

Frontend, Backend, Mobile & Full-Stack Development (2025–2026) — with a dedicated section for Lebanese students and developers.

TL;DR

  • For most fresh grads aiming at the global/remote market, Full-Stack (JavaScript: React + Node) offers the best ROI — broadest demand, cleanest single-language path, most freelance/remote-friendly. Backend pays highest; Frontend is the easiest entry; Mobile is a strong niche.
  • Realistic time-to-job-ready is 6–12 months (Frontend fastest at ~4–7 months; Full-Stack longest at ~8–12). A portfolio of real, deployed projects matters more than certificates.
  • For Lebanese developers, the winning move is remote work for international clients, not the local market. A local junior earns ~$800–$1,500/month; the same person working remotely for foreign clients can earn 3–4× that.
